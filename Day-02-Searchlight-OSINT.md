## 2. What Is OSINT?

OSINT stands for Open-Source Intelligence. It involves collecting and analysing information from publicly available sources for a specific purpose.

Examples of publicly available sources include:

- Search engines such as Google
- Public websites
- Online maps
- Public images and photographs
- Public reports and articles

OSINT is useful in cybersecurity investigations, threat intelligence, and digital research.

## 3. What Is IMINT?

IMINT stands for Image Intelligence. It involves analysing images to identify useful information.

During the Searchlight room, I learned that visual clues can help identify the possible location of an image.

These clues may include:

- Street signs and building names
- Landmarks and architecture
- Roads and surrounding objects
- Text visible in an image
- Other environmental details

Careful observation and verification are important when analysing images.
